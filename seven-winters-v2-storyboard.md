# SEVEN WINTERS / СЕМЬ ЗИМ — V2 storyboard

The edition is 26 pages and uses 16 photographs. The rhythm alternates Silence (A), Immersion (B), and Memory Fragment (C); no layout repeats for more than two consecutive spreads.

| Spread | Mode | Relationship | Treatment |
|---|---|---|---|
| 01—02 | A | title / place | Cropped monumental typography against tiny Moscow coordinates |
| 03—04 | A | year / arrival | Black 2019 field, then a hesitant airport frame |
| 05—06 | B | arrival / first view | Airport enlarged past the edge; rain fills the facing page |
| 07—08 | B→A | warm room / cold threshold | Offset dorm frame against the beginning of the snow panorama |
| 09—10 | B→C | winter / food | Snow panorama resolves; food breaks into overlapping fragments |
| 11—12 | C | public / together | Supermarket bands against grass and flash fragments |
| 13—14 | C→A | warmth / time | Birthday image at full force, followed by three sparse years |
| 15—16 | C→B | archive / metro | Three-frame contact strip against a severe vertical metro crop |
| 17—18 | B | movement / work | Sleeper train and bus fragment lead into the dark thesis desk |
| 19—20 | B | private / public | Thesis detail continues into the ordinary tram route |
| 21—22 | C→A | entrance / duration | Apartment number and hall, then near-silence |
| 23—24 | A→B | morning / final view | Small early window before the city expands across the gutter |
| 25—26 | B→A | city passing / archive end | Moving-car view resolves into a nearly empty back cover |

Selected files: 01, 02, 03, 04, 05, 08, 09, 10, 11, 12, 14, 15, 17, 18, 19, 20. The remaining photographs were removed to tighten the edit.
